Gentle Steps — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Gentle Steps helps you turn a goal into small steps with reminders. This page explains exactly what it stores, who else sees it, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to protect anyone legally.

The short version. Your tasks and goals are stored so the app can show them back to you and remind you. What you type as a goal is sent to Anthropic to be broken into steps. A short video ad from Google pays for that breakdown, and it is never chosen using anything you typed. Nothing is sold, and nothing you write in this app is tracked, analysed or used to target advertising. You can delete everything at any time.

What is stored

WhatWhy
Your email addressSo you can sign in and get back to your tasks on another device.
Your passwordStored only as a cryptographic hash by our authentication provider. Nobody, including us, can read it.
The goals you typeKept so the steps can be traced back to what you asked for.
Your tasks — title, date, time, whether it repeatsTo show your list and schedule reminders.
Which tasks you ticked off, and on which daySo a daily task can be done today and open again tomorrow.
How many breakdowns you have used this monthTo apply the monthly allowance.
Whether you have a paid subscription, and when it runs outTo take the ads away and raise your monthly allowance. Stored as a plan name and a date, nothing more.

That is the complete list of what this app stores. There is no location data, no contacts, and this app runs no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own. The app does not store your advertising ID — but Google does receive it when an ad is shown, and measures its own advertising with it. That is explained below.

Who else sees it

Anthropic — when you ask for a goal to be broken into steps, the text you typed is sent to Anthropic's API, which returns the ordered steps. This means the goal you write leaves this app. Please bear that in mind if you would write something you would not want processed by a third party. Anthropic does not use API data to train their models. Your email address and account are not sent — only the goal text and the current time.

Google AdMob — a short video ad plays before a free breakdown. That ad is what pays for the breakdown, which is how the free allowance can exist at all. To choose and count the ad, Google receives your device's advertising ID along with the usual technical details of any internet request — your IP address, device type and rough region. Google uses these to pick which ad you see, to measure how its own advertising performs, and to detect fraudulent clicks. That is Google measuring its advertising business, not this app watching what you do.

What Google never receives is anything you wrote. Your goals, your tasks, your email address and your account do not leave for Google, and no ad is targeted using them. You can reset or delete the advertising ID at any time in Android's Settings → Privacy → Ads, and the app carries on working. Ads are not shown on the paid plan.

RevenueCat — if you buy Gentle Steps Plus, RevenueCat is what checks with Google that the purchase is real and tells this app when your subscription starts, renews or ends. It receives your account's identifier and the purchase details Google gives it — which product, when it renews, whether it was refunded.

It does not receive your goals, your tasks or your email address, and it never sees your card. The setting that would let it collect your advertising ID and device identifiers is deliberately switched off, because this app has no use for it. If you never buy anything, RevenueCat is told nothing about you at all.

Supabase — hosts the database and handles sign-in. Your data is stored in their EU (Ireland) region.

Google Play — handles distribution, and takes the payment if you subscribe. Your card details go to Google and never reach this app or RevenueCat; we are told only that a payment succeeded. Google does not receive your tasks. You can cancel a subscription at any time in the Play Store.

Nothing is sold or rented, and no data broker is involved. Google is the only advertiser, and it receives none of your goals, tasks or account details — only the advertising ID and technical request details described above.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your own phone by Android, not sent from a server. The app does not use push notifications and holds no push token. If you deny or withdraw notification permission, everything else keeps working — you just will not be nudged.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can delete individual tasks in the app at any time.

To delete your whole account and everything attached to it, email the address below from the address you signed up with, and it will be erased within 30 days. Deletion removes your tasks, goals, completions and account record — it cannot be undone.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected, ask for it to be deleted, or object to how it is handled. Use the contact address below and you will get a reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

Gentle Steps is not designed for or aimed at children under 13, and accounts should not be created for them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who sees it, the app will say so rather than quietly updating this page.
