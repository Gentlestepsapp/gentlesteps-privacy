You already know what needs doing.
Starting is the hard part.

Write it in your own words. Gentle Steps turns it into at most three small steps, in the order they need to happen, each with a reminder that checks back a few minutes later: have you started?

Get it on Google Play

Free on Android. For adults, 18+. An app for people with ADHD, autism or dyslexia, and for anyone who finds starting the hard part.

Twenty-eight seconds. Narrated by an AI voice; the app screens are the real app.

What it does

Three steps, in order

Type what you want done. It comes back as small steps, at most three, in the order they actually need to happen, each with a time.

A reminder that checks back

Every step with a time gets a real notification. A little later, a quiet follow-up asks whether you managed to start, after 2, 5, 10 or 20 minutes, or not at all. You choose.

Kind about a missed day

No streaks to break, no completion percentages, no points. On a hard day, tap Need a hand? to keep one step and move the rest to tomorrow.

The tips in the app's Learn tab each say plainly how strong the research behind them is, including where it is weak. The studies, one by one.

Free, with an optional subscription

The free version is paid for by a short video advert before each set of steps. Gentle Steps Plus removes the adverts and adds a tidy week already decided, bin reminders that know your collection day, four ways to shrink a task you're stuck on, a way to rework a day that's too much, planning something bigger across the week, and your next step on your home screen.

If you cancel, Plus runs to the date you have already paid for. If you subscribe and don't use it, email and ask: you get back the time you have not used. Either way the free version keeps your tasks, your reminders and both reading settings.
