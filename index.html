Gentle Steps — Privacy Policy Last updated: 4 September 2026 Gentle Steps helps you turn a goal into small steps with reminders. This page explains exactly what it stores, who else sees it, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to protect anyone legally. The short version. Your tasks and goals are stored so the app can show them back to you and remind you. What you type as a goal is sent to Anthropic to be broken into steps. A short video ad from Google pays for that breakdown, and it is never chosen using anything you typed. Nothing is sold and there is no tracking or analytics of any kind. You can delete everything at any time. What is stored What Why Your email address So you can sign in and get back to your tasks on another device. Your password Stored only as a cryptographic hash by our authentication provider. Nobody, including us, can read it. The goals you type Kept so the steps can be traced back to what you asked for. Your tasks — title, date, time, whether it repeats To show your list and schedule reminders. Which tasks you ticked off, and on which day So a daily task can be done today and open again tomorrow. How many breakdowns you have used this month To apply the monthly allowance. That is the complete list of what this app stores. There is no location data, no contacts, and no analytics or crash-reporting service. The app does not store your advertising ID — but Google does receive it when an ad is shown, which is explained below. Who else sees it Anthropic — when you ask for a goal to be broken into steps, the text you typed is sent to Anthropic's API, which returns the ordered steps. This means the goal you write leaves this app. Please bear that in mind if you would write something you would not want processed by a third party. Anthropic does not use API data to train their models. Your email address and account are not sent — only the goal text and the current time. Google AdMob — a short video ad plays before a free breakdown. That ad is what pays for the breakdown, which is how the free allowance can exist at all. To choose and count the ad, Google receives your device's advertising ID along with the usual technical details of any internet request — your IP address, device type and rough region. Google may use these to pick which ad you see. What Google never receives is anything you wrote. Your goals, your tasks, your email address and your account do not leave for Google, and no ad is targeted using them. You can reset or delete the advertising ID at any time in Android's Settings → Privacy → Ads, and the app carries on working. Ads are not shown on the paid plan. Supabase — hosts the database and handles sign-in. Your data is stored in their EU (Ireland) region. Google Play — handles distribution. Google does not receive your tasks. Nothing is sold or rented, and no data broker is involved. Google is the only advertiser, and it receives none of your goals, tasks or account details — only the advertising ID and technical request details described above. Reminders Reminders are scheduled on your own phone by Android, not sent from a server. The app does not use push notifications and holds no push token. If you deny or withdraw notification permission, everything else keeps working — you just will not be nudged. Keeping and deleting your data Your data is kept until you delete it. You can delete individual tasks in the app at any time. To delete your whole account and everything attached to it, email the address below from the address you signed up with, and it will be erased within 30 days. Deletion removes your tasks, goals, completions and account record — it cannot be undone. Your rights Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected, ask for it to be deleted, or object to how it is handled. Use the contact address below and you will get a reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. Children Gentle Steps is not designed for or aimed at children under 13, and accounts should not be created for them. Changes If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who sees it, the app will say so rather than quietly updating this page. Contact gentlestepsapp@gmail.com
